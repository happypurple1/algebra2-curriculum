## TEKS Coverage

These materials align with the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills (TEKS) for Algebra 2, covering:
- Functions, notation, domain and range, and parent functions
- Absolute value equations and transformations
- Systems of equations (linear, nonlinear, and matrices)
- Quadratic functions, factoring, and solving methods
- Radical expressions and equations
- Polynomial operations and analysis
- Rational functions and expressions
- Exponential and logarithmic functions

## Differentiation

Materials are provided at two levels:
- **Advanced**: Extended problems, deeper conceptual exploration, and more rigorous assessments
- **On-Level**: Scaffolded instruction with additional practice and guided notes
